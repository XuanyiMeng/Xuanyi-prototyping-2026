# Stardew Valley Style Pink Room Mini-Game 🌾🌸

An interactive 2D RPG mini-game prototype built with **PixiJS v8** and Next.js, featuring Stardew Valley & Sims style HUD elements and interactive room mechanics.

## Features
- **Map & Character**: Pink aesthetic room (wallpaper, rug, bed, study desk with homework) with a cute chibi girl avatar.
- **Movement**:
  - `WASD` or Arrow keys for 4-directional movement.
  - Mouse click on room floor to pathfind/walk to target.
- **Interactions**:
  - **Study Desk**: Click desk when nearby to pull out a giant hammer and smash the desk & homework with flames, debris, and screen shake!
  - **Pink Bed**: Click bed when nearby to hop into bed, trigger floating `Zzz...` particles, and restore energy bar to 100%.
- **Stardew Valley HUD & Hotbar**:
  - Gold Counter (`💰 1,250g`).
  - Energy/Stamina Bar (`⚡ 体力值`).
  - Day & Time Display (`📅 第 1 天 09:00 AM`).
  - Bottom Inventory Hotbar with selectable items (🔨 锤子, 🍿 零食, 🪓 木斧, 🪴 浇水壶). Clicking snacks restores energy!
