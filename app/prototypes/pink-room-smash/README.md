# Pink Room Homework Smash 🔨🌸

An interactive 2D room prototype built with **PixiJS v8** and Next.js.

## Features
- **Character**: Cute chibi girl avatar matching Image 1 (black hair, pink rimmed glasses, polka dot top, pink notebook).
- **Pink Aesthetic Room**: Soft pastel pink room interior with wallpaper, bed, sofa, rug, and study desk.
- **Controls**:
  - `WASD` / Arrow Keys to move around the room.
  - Mouse Click to pathfind / walk to any position on the floor.
  - Proximity detection: Walk up to the Study Desk to trigger the interaction prompt!
- **Interactive Smash Action**:
  - Press `Space` or Click "砸碎作业！(Smash Homework!)".
  - Character's head bursts into animated flame particles!
  - Character pulls out a giant toy mallet hammer!
  - Smashes the desk into flying paper sheets, homework debris, and dust particles with screen shake!
  - "重置书桌 (Reset Desk)" button to rebuild the desk and smash again.
